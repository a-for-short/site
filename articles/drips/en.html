Everyone is already aware of drip packets, right?
As I see it, the trick is that they are compact and can be used anywhere, having boiling water and a mug, to brew coffee from good beans of your choice. They are also stored under nitrogen from the moment of grinding, so you get an excellent quality drink anywhere with minimal effort.
