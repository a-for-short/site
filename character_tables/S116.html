       This is essential when trying to decompose a reducible representation into "irreducible" ones using the familiar projection formula.

    ζ  Some of the characters in the table are irrational because the order of the principal axis is neither 1,2,3,4 nor 6.
       These irrational values can be expressed as cosine values, or as solutions of algebraic equations with a leading coefficient of 1.
       All characters are algebraic integers of a degree less than half the order of the principal axis (and often much less).
       For this group, however, none of the characters can be expressed by a closed algebraic form using real numbers only.
