    β  The C100h point group is generated by two symmetry elements, which are canonically chosen as C100 and i.

    γ  The lowest nonvanishing multipole moment in C100h is 4 (quadrupole moment).

    ε  Because the group is Abelian and the maximum order of rotation is >2, some irreducible representations have complex characters.
