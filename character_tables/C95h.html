 Notes:

    α  The order of the C95h point group is 190, and the order of the principal axis is 190.

    β  The C95h point group could also be named S95, as it contains the S95 axis as its only symmetry element.
       Another rare designation is C190i because the S95 axis is identical to a roto-inversion axis of order 190.

    γ  This is an Abelian point group (the commutative law holds between all symmetry operations).
       The C95h group is Abelian because it contains only one symmetry element, all the powers of which necessarily commute (sufficient condition).
       In Abelian groups, all symmetry operations form a class of their own, and all irreducible representations are one-dimensional.

    δ  Because the group is Abelian and the maximum order of rotation is >2, some irreducible representations have complex characters.
       These 188 cases have been combined into 94 two-dimensional representations that are no longer irreducible but have real-valued characters.
       Accordingly, 94 pairs of left and right rotations have been combined into one two-membered pseudo-class each.

    ε  The 94 reducible "E" representations almost behave like true irreducible representations.
       Their norm, however, is twice the group order. Therefore, they have been marked with an asterisk in the table.
       This is essential when trying to decompose a reducible representation into "irreducible" ones using the familiar projection formula.

    ζ  Some of the characters in the table are irrational because the order of the principal axis is neither 1,2,3,4 nor 6.
       These irrational values can be expressed as cosine values, or as solutions of algebraic equations with a leading coefficient of 1.
       All characters are algebraic integers of a degree less than half the order of the principal axis (and often much less).
       For this group, however, some of the characters cannot be expressed by a closed algebraic form using real numbers only.
