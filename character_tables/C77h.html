    γ  The C77h point group is isomorphic to C154 and S154.

    δ  The C77h point group is generated by one single symmetry element, S77. Therefore, it is a cyclic group.

    ε  The lowest nonvanishing multipole moment in C77h is 4 (quadrupole moment).
